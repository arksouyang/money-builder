# H02: Napoleon wasn't short

- **Story:** Napoleon's height myth and British propaganda
- **Target length:** ~45 s (~140 words)
- **Title:** Napoleon wasn't short. He lost a meme war.
- **Hook text on screen:** "Napoleon was average height"

---

**HOOK (0–3 s)**
Napoleon wasn't short. He got roasted by British cartoonists.

**BEAT 1: the units**
When he died, his height was written down as five foot two. But that was in *French* units, and the French inch was longer than the English one. In modern terms he was about five-six, five-seven. Pretty average for a Frenchman back then.

**BEAT 2: the cartoons**
Then there's James Gillray, a British cartoonist who drew him as a tiny, tantrum-throwing "Little Boney." Britain was at war with him, so the joke spread everywhere.

**BEAT 3: bad luck**
It didn't help that his soldiers' nickname for him was "the little corporal," which was actually affectionate. Or that he was usually surrounded by his Imperial Guard, who had to be tall to get in.

**ENDING**
So the guy who ran most of Europe lost one war: the meme war. Two hundred years later, we still say "Napoleon complex."
