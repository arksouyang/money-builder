# H05: Cleopatra lived closer to the Moon landing than to the pyramids

- **Story:** time-scale fact + who Cleopatra actually was
- **Target length:** ~45 s (~140 words)
- **Title:** Cleopatra is closer to us than to the pyramids
- **Hook text on screen:** "Pyramids → Cleopatra: 2,500 yrs. Cleopatra → Moon: 2,000 yrs."

---

**HOOK (0–3 s)**
Cleopatra lived closer in time to the Moon landing than to the building of the Great Pyramid.

**BEAT 1: the math**
The Great Pyramid was finished around 2560 BC. Cleopatra died in 30 BC. That's about 2,500 years. From Cleopatra to Neil Armstrong? About 2,000.

**BEAT 2: what that means**
To Cleopatra, the pyramids were already ancient. They were as old to her as Confucius is to us.

**BEAT 3: who she was**
And she wasn't ethnically Egyptian. Her family, the Ptolemies, were Greek-Macedonian, descended from one of Alexander the Great's generals. They ruled Egypt for almost 300 years, and Cleopatra was reportedly the first one in the family to actually learn Egyptian.

**ENDING**
Ancient history isn't one big blob. Some of it was ancient even to the ancients. Follow for more history that breaks your sense of time.
