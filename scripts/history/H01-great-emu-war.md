# H01: The Great Emu War

- **Story:** Western Australia, Nov–Dec 1932
- **Target length:** ~50 s (~150 words)
- **Title:** Australia went to war with birds and lost
- **Hook text on screen:** "Australia vs. emus (1932)"

---

**HOOK (0–3 s)**
In 1932, Australia went to war with birds. And lost.

**BEAT 1: the problem**
After World War One, Australia gave veterans farmland in Western Australia. Then around 20,000 emus showed up and started eating the wheat.

**BEAT 2: the army**
So the government sent the army: Major Meredith, two machine guns, and ten thousand rounds of ammo. Turns out emus run about 30 miles an hour, scatter the second you shoot, and can take a hit and keep running. One gun jammed. They tried mounting a gun on a truck. The truck couldn't keep up.

**BEAT 3: the score**
After about a month, Meredith claimed 986 kills using almost 10,000 bullets. That's ten bullets per bird. He reportedly said that if he had a military division with the bullet-carrying capacity of these birds, it would face any army in the world.

**ENDING**
The army pulled out. The emus stayed. Farmers went back to paying bounties on emus, which worked way better. Final score: Australia zero, emus one.
